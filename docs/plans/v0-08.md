# V0-08 — founder-ready decision loop

Status: implementation, founder proof and bounded independent review complete;
pending stacked draft PR delivery and exact-head CI on
`codex/v0-08-founder-decision-loop`.

Base: accepted V0-07 head `149f93cd43092996e20621e8976003b341a82c6a`.

This slice turns V0-07's evidence-correct product understanding into a coherent recursive purchase-decision experience. It preserves the accepted shopping-state, retrieval, evidence and assessment boundaries. It does not create ProductIdentity, a crawler, global preference learning, authentication, checkout, affiliate ranking or a generic agent framework.

## Product outcome

The founder can:

1. describe a real purchase naturally;
2. see useful UK listings without waiting for evidence research;
3. watch a bounded first evidence pass enrich a promising pool;
4. understand whether each product is ready to consider, needs verification, has a trade-off or conflicts with a must-have;
5. investigate only gaps that could change eligibility, ordering or a saved comparison;
6. save products, compare them, reject one exact task-local listing and undo that rejection;
7. refine the brief and receive reassessment against the new authoritative revision;
8. leave through an attributable retailer or Google Shopping fallback.

## Decision semantics

`UNKNOWN` remains separate from `CONFLICT`.

Ordering is deterministic and considers, in order:

1. evidenced hard conflicts;
2. unresolved hard requirements;
3. supported hard requirements;
4. stronger preference conflicts and support;
5. ordinary preference conflicts and support;
6. non-hard unknowns;
7. known target-distance relationships;
8. a stable task-local identifier.

Readiness is consumer-facing and has no score:

- `qualified`: no hard conflict or unresolved hard requirement;
- `needs_verification`: no hard conflict, but at least one hard requirement remains unknown or not applicable;
- `trade_off`: hard requirements are resolved but an evidenced softer preference conflicts;
- `ineligible`: an evidenced hard conflict or absolute purchase-boundary breach exists.

The strongest-supported badge is unavailable unless at least one candidate has resolved its hard requirements. Absence of evidence can never make a product purchase-ready.

## Progressive research policy

Policy version: `evidence-progressive-v2`.

First pass:

- maximum four exact task-local candidate listings;
- one combined organic evidence search per candidate;
- one product-understanding call per candidate;
- direct listing evidence is retained and attributed;
- visible factual listings are never blocked on completion.

Deepening:

- normally the leading or saved two candidates, never more than three in one plan;
- maximum two unresolved criteria per candidate and search;
- hard gaps before strong preferences before ordinary preferences;
- exact candidate title plus the selected criterion labels;
- completed criterion searches are excluded from later plans;
- concurrent automatic and manual triggers converge on the same persisted policy identity;
- partial runs resume only planned work;
- prior current assessments stay valid if later research fails.

Automatic deepening runs once per mounted current view after first-pass evidence. Further candidate-level investigation is deliberate and may move to a new unresolved criterion, but never repeats a completed criterion search. Browser triggers carry only the founder session and, for deliberate investigation, an exact listing identifier. Task, revision, research-run and policy identities remain server-owned.

Refinement creates a new authoritative shopping revision through V0-05/V0-04. Historical exact-listing observations may be reused, while assessments are appended for the new current revision. New evidence is allocated according to the refined hard/strong gaps.

## Research state and failure

Research runs and attempts remain persisted, leased with PostgreSQL time and fenced. Provider/model calls remain outside database transactions.

Candidate research is projected as:

- `available`: a current important unresolved criterion has not been attempted;
- `researching`: a deepening run for a current gap is still active;
- `complete`: every current unresolved criterion in scope has received its bounded focused attempt, even if truth remains unknown;
- `failed`: the focused attempt failed and earlier evidence remains intact.

This prevents a visible no-op “Research this more” action after a completed attempt.

## Exact listing rejection

`Not for me` means only that the shopper does not want one exact `CandidateListing` in one task.

- task-local private row;
- idempotent reject;
- reject and removal from Saved are atomic;
- undo removes rejection but does not silently re-save;
- refresh preserves current rejection;
- rejection never changes `ShoppingTask.currentRevision`, `ShoppingBrief`, `DecisionCriterion` or model memory;
- rejected listings are excluded from current shortlist, research and saved comparison;
- a collapsed disclosure preserves recovery;
- cross-task writes fail closed.

An explained dislike remains an ordinary explicit natural-language refinement and enters through V0-05; the button itself never learns a preference.

## Comparison

The saved comparison is derived only from current persisted criteria, assessments and linked evidence. It presents:

- must-have status per saved option;
- actual evidenced differences rather than a generic leader statement;
- important unknowns ordered by criterion authority;
- observed price, its persisted target relationship and attributable purchase destination;
- a full criterion-by-criterion evidence table on demand.

At most four exact task-local listings may be saved. The limit is enforced
under the task lock, so concurrent saves cannot exceed it. Every saved listing
remains visible in comparison: assessed options show current evidence, while an
unassessed option shows unknown cells and an explicit investigation path rather
than disappearing.

## Independent-review corrections

The final bounded review found four material boundary issues, all corrected
without broadening the slice:

- an assessment may cite only an observation emitted for that exact criterion;
  provider lowering, policy guards and persistence all fail closed on
  cross-concept evidence;
- the persisted offer price is authoritative only for purchase-price criteria;
  delivery, running and other money concepts require their own concept-scoped
  evidence;
- all saved listings remain visible and removable in comparison, including
  unassessed listings, with an atomic four-listing task cap;
- provider, persisted research-target and authoritative criterion limits now
  agree at 50, with a 13-criterion PostgreSQL regression proving the previous
  hidden truncation boundary is gone.

Decision copy may say that one candidate has stronger support only when the corresponding assessment cells establish that difference. It cannot introduce product facts.

## Destination cost boundary

V0-08 measures the Serper shopping request separately from its conservative merchant-resolution requests. V0-07's verified destination provenance is retained. Moving destination enrichment after shortlisting would require a new immutable enrichment persistence boundary; this slice will not disguise that change as a small adapter edit. The release evidence reports direct-retailer coverage among top and saved options and the actual extra request cost, so the next checkpoint can make the decision from observed value.

The first V0-08 diagnostic spent 25 merchant-resolution requests and produced a
direct destination for only one of eleven visible top options. The bounded
correction now checks only the leading distinct merchant per shopping query.
The exact-code release proof used 9 merchant-resolution requests across 12
shopping queries. It retained 1/4 top and 0/2 saved direct coverage for the
mouse, while chair and vacuum honestly retained Google Shopping fallbacks. A
post-shortlist resolver is deferred because it needs its own immutable,
idempotent provenance boundary rather than an in-place listing mutation.

## Founder evidence

Fresh disposable-database journeys cover:

- the established ergonomic mouse request and refinement;
- the established office-chair request with conditional stretch;
- a materially different cordless-vacuum request stressing floor compatibility, noise, weight and runtime.

Each journey records retrieval queries, raw candidates, promising candidates, first/deep evidence calls, product-understanding calls, hard unknowns before/after, saved/rejected state, top/saved direct retailer coverage, time to initial listings, time to decision support and the honest point where the founder would still leave Consider.

The proof artifacts are sanitized JSON and Markdown under `docs/evals/`. Screenshots under `docs/screenshots/v0-08/` cover desktop and 390 × 844 mobile states, with horizontal-overflow assertions.

Across mouse plus chair, the release proof used 20 evidence searches versus
V0-07's 40 (50% fewer). It used 22 product-understanding calls versus 20 (10%
more) because the loop now performs bounded first pass, decision-gap deepening,
candidate research and a real mouse refinement. That is a deliberate exchange:
fewer broad web calls for more selective, revision-aware judgement. The third
vacuum journey added 7 evidence searches and 7 understanding calls. Important
unknowns remained unknown when the bounded sources could not resolve them.

The founder leave-the-app test remains honest. Direct destination coverage is
still thin; search snippets do not settle personal chair comfort, brand
reputation, vacuum noise, floor compatibility or durability. Those measured
gaps—not a desire for broader autonomous crawling—should determine the next
checkpoint after V0-08 acceptance.
